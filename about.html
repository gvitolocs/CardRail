Card Rails is a scanning desk and stock book for collectible-card inventory. Scan cards with your phone, give every copy a shelf position, and keep stock and sales in one book.

Open the desk → Source on GitHub

Card Rails is a working project, built in the open. Features may change, and there are no plans or prices.

How it works

From phone to shelf to sale.

Four stops on one line. Each one is in the app today.

  1. 01

    Scan on your phone

    Open the desk and pair your phone by scanning its QR code, or type the 4-digit code at cardrails.vercel.app/connect and approve it on the desk. Every photo you take is saved with its scan.

  2. 02

    Recognise

    Pokémon scans are matched against the public Pokoin catalog. When a match is unsure, pick the right card from the candidates, or search the catalog and add a card by hand.

  3. 03

    Place on a shelf

    Confirm the batch and each copy gets its own shelf position, like B05-R1-2100: box, row, position. Each position has a QR and barcode shelf label.

  4. 04

    Sell and pick

    Connect CardTrader or eBay from Platforms. A sale books the copy out once, and the pick run lists every sold card by shelf position.

Features

What's on the desk today.

Phone pairing

Pair with a QR code or a 4-digit code. Typed codes need approval on the desk; QR links expire after ten minutes and work once.

Saved scan photos

Every scan keeps its photo. When you publish a card to eBay, the listing uses that original photo.

Batch defaults

Set game, language, condition (NM, SP, MP, PL, Poor), finish, 1st edition, signed, altered and location once for the whole batch.

Shelf positions

Card Rails reserves one physical position per copy, across stacks, so inventory always says where a card sits.

Sale or collection

List a batch for sale or add it to your collection. Collection cards hide their price and can't be sold or published.

Book

Every sale is a line: quantity out, shelf code and channel. The same card is not booked twice for one order.

Pick run

Sold cards become a picking list sorted by shelf position, with a walk view that goes card by card.

Live stock

One account with the iPhone and Android apps. CardTrader products import in the background; locations come from the scanner or a stock CSV.

Developer API

Create read-only API keys to read saved cards, shelf locations and the stock book. Keys can't change stock or publish listings.

Platforms

Where your stock goes.

Card Rails keeps the inventory. These are the channels it talks to today.

CardTrader

Listings and orders

Connect with a verified API token, stored encrypted. Import your products, map existing listings, send stock changes as deltas and receive signed sale webhooks.

eBay

Listings

Sign in with eBay, use your own shipping, payment and return policies, and publish a card with its saved scan photo.

Pokoin

Catalog and recognition

Public card catalog search and scan recognition. Seller sync is not available yet: it needs a Card Rails grant from Pokoin.
